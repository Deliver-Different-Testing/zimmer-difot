# Zimmer Biomet communications summary (16 July 2026 - 25 August 2026)

## Purpose

This note summarises the Zimmer Biomet commercial communications trail from 16 July 2026 through to Marcus's follow-up on 25 August 2026, including the meeting setup with Mark Mottram, the proposal follow-up, the attached draft Heads of Agreement, and the current position.

## Current position

- Urgent sought a meeting with Mark Mottram from 16 July onward after learning the move to Smartways was underway.
- Internal pricing work concluded Urgent could credibly offer around **$20,000 per month** in savings, but not materially more without wiping out margin or cutting courier pay to an unsustainable level.
- Steve escalated formally to Mark on 28 July and then to Warren Ballinger on 31 July after repeated attempts to get a response.
- Mark replied on 31 July and agreed to a meeting time.
- After the meeting, Marcus sent a written proposal summary and a draft **Heads of Agreement** built around a **90-day demonstration period**.
- **No written feedback has been received from Mark on either the proposal or the draft HOA.**
- Per Steve's update on 25 August, Urgent has continued speaking with **Greg Cheesman**, who put a business case to Mark to keep the work with Urgent. Greg initially indicated Mark would allow that, but yesterday Greg reported that Mark had changed position and was asking why Greg was so biased toward Urgent.
- Marcus followed up again on **25 August 2026**, advising that because three weeks had passed without volume recovery, Urgent would proceed with the rate adjustment from **Monday 31 August 2026** unless Zimmer re-engaged.

## Chronology

### 16 July 2026 - Steve requests a meeting with Mark

Steve emailed Mark Mottram requesting a 30-minute meeting before the transition affected urgent, theatre-critical services.

Key points raised:
- Smartways' exit from another major device company in Australia and New Zealand.
- Urgent's willingness to match or beat the commercial outcome if Zimmer shared what was driving the move.
- The transition risk and cost implications for Zimmer if Urgent's network consolidated around reduced volume.

Steve offered times on 17, 20, and 21 July.

### 17 July 2026 - Internal pricing analysis sets the commercial limit

Marcus circulated internal pricing analysis showing:
- Urgent could offer savings, but the realistic ceiling was about **$20,000 per month**.
- A **15% invoice reduction** looked like the most realistic offer while keeping group margin slightly positive.
- A higher concession would either remove margin entirely or require a material reduction in courier pay.

Marcus later added that linehaul contribution was also meaningful, estimating roughly:
- **~$9.3k/month air linehaul GP**
- **~$14.8k/month total linehaul-only contribution**

### 28 July 2026 - Steve sends formal savings proposal to Mark

After stating he had emailed, texted, and called over the prior 10 days without response, Steve sent a consolidated note to Mark titled **"Zimmer Biomet Partnership Significant Savings Opportunity"**.

Core points:
- Urgent wanted to preserve the 20-year NZ partnership.
- Urgent was willing to offer **$15k-$20k per month** in savings on the NZ work.
- Steve urged Mark to speak with the former LifeHealthcare operations leader about Smartways' prior exit.
- Steve challenged Zimmer to test Smartways' Auckland operating depth against Urgent's own network scale.
- Steve attached a **transition pricing schedule effective 1 August** because the original pricing depended on full-account volume.
- Steve asked for a call that week and warned that if there was no response, Urgent would proceed with the new pricing the following Monday.

### 28 July 2026 - Internal discussion about exit timing

Marcus circulated internal material on the Zimmer exit timeline, including a note that **Gisborne was meant to be week 8**. Deane then asked whether he should chase Greg on the timeline.

### 31 July 2026 - Escalation to Warren Ballinger

With no response from Mark, Steve forwarded the 28 July proposal to Warren Ballinger and copied Mark, Greg, Marcus, and Deane.

The escalation said:
- Urgent had carried Zimmer's critical freight in NZ for close to 20 years.
- Urgent had offered to keep the work with savings of around **$20,000 per month**.
- A transition had already begun without notice.
- The last two weeks had run at around **60% of baseline volume**.
- Under the previously supplied schedule, a **30% rate adjustment** would trigger on **Monday 3 August** for the remaining services.

Warren's only written reply was an out-of-office auto-response.

### 31 July 2026 - Mark responds and offers a meeting time

Mark replied saying:
- he looked after Commercial Operations in Australia/New Zealand including freight and transport contracts;
- he had seen Steve's Tuesday email, but not earlier messages; and
- he could speak at **2pm Monday** or **10am Tuesday Sydney time**.

Steve then confirmed **Tuesday 10am Sydney time**.

### 4-5 August 2026 - Meeting held and Marcus sends proposal follow-up + draft HOA

Following the meeting with Mark, Marcus sent a written follow-up summarising the proposal and attaching / linking the draft **Heads of Agreement**.

The written offer said:
- Zimmer would save approximately **$20,000 per month** once the work returned to Urgent.
- Operationally Zimmer would retain the incumbent provider, same drivers, technologies, and service levels.
- The draft HOA created a **90-day demonstration period** so Urgent could prove the saving before a longer-term commitment was locked in.
- If Urgent failed to deliver the promised saving, the agreement would **not be binding**.

Next steps proposed by Marcus:
1. Mark's comments on the draft HOA, ideally signed early the following week.
2. Transition back to Urgent from the week of **10 August**.
3. Use the 90-day period to work through the long-form agreement, KPI reporting, and systems integration.

This is the key written proposal sent after the meeting.

### 6 August 2026 - Internal Greg planning session

Marcus sent an internal calendar invite titled **"Zimmer - Greg Plan"** for 6 August, indicating Urgent was actively coordinating next steps around Greg's involvement.

### 13 August 2026 - Internal Zimmer operational review

A Fathom recap titled **"Zimmer"** records an internal operational discussion about:
- missed linehaul cut-off warning behaviour,
- outdated schedules,
- child-job timing bugs,
- incorrect POD display logic.

This was not a commercial response from Mark, but it does show Urgent continued reviewing Zimmer-related service issues internally during the period of silence.

### 25 August 2026 - Marcus follows up again after silence

Marcus emailed Mark again, copying Steve, Deane, and Greg.

Key points:
- Urgent had still received **no feedback** on the proposal or the draft HOA.
- Because three weeks had passed and volume had not recovered, Urgent would proceed with the **rate adjustment from Monday 31 August 2026**.
- The proposal remained open and Marcus invited Mark to meet again if he wanted to discuss it.

## Verbal / off-email position reported to Steve

Based on Steve's 25 August update:
- Urgent has been communicating with **Greg Cheesman** during the silence from Mark.
- Greg put a business case to Mark supporting Zimmer staying with Urgent.
- Greg initially indicated Mark would let that happen.
- Greg then reported yesterday that Mark had changed position and was asking why Greg was so biased toward Urgent.

This is important context because it suggests the commercial position may have shifted internally at Zimmer even though **no equivalent written feedback has been provided to Urgent directly**.

## Attached HOA - role in the trail

The draft HOA sent after the meeting was positioned as:
- a short-form commercial bridge,
- a **90-day proof period**,
- a mechanism for immediate savings delivery,
- and a way to defer a long-term binding commitment until Zimmer had evidence in hand.

In practical terms, Urgent's written offer was not just "give us the work back". It was:
- immediate monthly savings,
- incumbent operational continuity,
- low commitment risk for Zimmer,
- and a structured path into a longer-term agreement.

## Overall read

The comms trail shows a consistent pattern:
- Urgent repeatedly tried to get engagement before and after the Smartways transition.
- Once contact was made, Urgent put forward a commercially meaningful, structured proposal.
- The proposal was then documented in writing and supported by a draft HOA.
- **Mark has not provided written feedback on the proposal or HOA.**
- Greg appears to have advocated internally for Urgent, but the internal Zimmer position has become less favourable based on his latest report.
- Marcus's 25 August email is effectively a final nudge before the rate change proceeds.

## Key documentary points for any future reference

If this trail needs to be used in a board note, negotiation summary, or legal/commercial pack, the most important evidential points are:
- early and repeated attempts to engage Mark;
- the explicit **$15k-$20k/month** savings proposal;
- warning of transition pricing consequences as volume fell;
- Mark's acknowledgment and meeting acceptance;
- the post-meeting written proposal and draft HOA;
- the absence of any written response from Mark on substance;
- the 25 August follow-up giving notice of the 31 August rate adjustment.
